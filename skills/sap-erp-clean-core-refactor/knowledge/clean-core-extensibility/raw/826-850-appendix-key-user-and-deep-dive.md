Monitor and administrate
Combined monitoring of deployed workflows and automation jobs, offering
several interlinked views for Process and Workflow Instances, Automation Jobs,
and Acquired Events
Monitor
• Process and Workflow Instances: see a list of all workflow instances and act on those
instances
• Automation Jobs: view all your automation errors, warnings, variables, and other information
on the console
• Acquired Events: view the events that have been acquired by the process visibility capability
• Automation Overview: shows an overview of the status of your agents and reviews historical
data
Manage
• Processes and Workflow Definitions: shows a list of deployed processes and workflow
definitions
• Triggers: shows you information about where triggers are used and allows you to manage
triggers.
• Visibility Scenarios: manage your visibility scenarios by scheduling the job for processing the
data and clearing the processed data. You can also view the processing log information and the
details of a scenario definition in this view.
• Business Rules: launch the Manage Rule Projects (MRP) application and perform advanced
configurations on business rules projects that are imported as part of live process packages.
